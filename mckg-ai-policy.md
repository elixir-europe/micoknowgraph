## Rules

- It is **permitted** to use generative AI for MCKG repository contents clearly stating:
  - which part are generated by which AI model.
  - clearly indicate the AI generative model used and its version.
  - transparently indicate if and which parts have been reviewed after AI generation.
  - for human-genAI combination please briefly describe the workflow you used.

- LLMs **may be used** to analyze, distill, refine, check, suggest and review.
  - clearly indicate the LLM used and its version.
  - transparetly track if the contribution is from LLM, human or from combination of them.
  - for human-LLM combination please briefly describe the workflow you used.

- Code, text, documentation contributions should be provided by tacking care of few requirements:
  - you should be able to furnish rationale for every line of code in your contribution.
  - you must be able to **answer about rationale and technical questions in review** without AI-LLM help and make any requested changes.
  - you must **disclose and describe** the use of generative AI/LLM in your PR description.

- Every **contributor is responsible** for ensuring that their contribution does not violate our policy or license
- Since AI generated information is frequently misleading or false, we cannot offer support on anything referencing AI output.
- Maintainers are encouraged to deprioritise or reject contributions that they suspect have been made in violation of this policy.


## Motivation

We believe that Human/AI fingerprint traceability in contribution of this project may critically help to improve FAIRness of the final product we will release and maintain. AI algorithms have FAIRness problems as some articles try to depict [AI FAIRness](
https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.14636).

### Points to be taken in consideration

LLMs [distort our written language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18161), reduce [linguistic diversity](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02550-0) and result in [semantic ablation](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/02/16/semantic-ablation-why-ai-writing-is-boring-and-dangerous/4414930), which is highly detrimental.
Diverse authors bring diverse viewpoints which LLMs may homogenize because of its attitude to by definition make homogeneous all of the text they edit.


## Societal Motivation

AI tools have numerous ethical issues. [Modern AI tools are unlawful by design](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/pol40/0996/2026/en/) per human rights organisation Amnesty International. [They require an unreasonable amount of energy](https://www.theverge.com/2024/5/15/24157496/microsoft-ai-carbon-footprint-greenhouse-gas-emissions-grow-climate-pledge), and [water](https://restofworld.org/2024/data-centers-environmental-issues/)  to build and operate, their models are built in part with heavily [exploited workers in unacceptable working conditions](https://time.com/6247678/openai-chatgpt-kenya-workers/), usually [without consent from or compensation for creators of the source material](https://variety.com/vip-special-reports/ai-training-consent-content-licensing-special-report-1236307878/). Furthermore, they are being used to [undermine labor and justify layoffs](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richardnieva/2025/07/17/ai-tech-layoffs/). Even if one accepts the premise they are useful, they bias [high income countries and contribute to a widening skills gap](https://doi.org/10.1126%2Fscience.adz9311). And generally are contrary to the [fundamental reasons we do science](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/15/why-are-we-even-doing-all-this/) and we share our knowledge with others.

These are harms that we do not want to perpetuate, even if only indirectly.
